{% elif page == 'guidelines' %}

Welcome to PARALLAX 2026! We are delighted to have you join us for 24 hours of innovation, collaboration, and problem-solving. To ensure a smooth, fair, and enjoyable experience for all participants, kindly read and adhere to the following guidelines.

Eligibility

  • PARALLAX 2026 is open to students from all branches and all years of Engineering.
  • Teams may comprise both VIT and Non-VIT participants.
  • Cross-college teams are permitted and encouraged.

Team Formation

  • Each team must consist of 3 to 5 members.
  • During registration, every team must select one official hackathon problem statement of the desired domain through the website.
  • The submitted project must align with the selected domain.

Problem Statement Release

  • Hardware problem statements will be released 108 hours prior to the commencement of the hackathon.
  • Software problem statements will be released 36 hours prior to the commencement of the hackathon.

Reporting & Verification

  • All participants are requested to report to the venue by 12:30 PM for registration and verification.
  • Every participant must carry a valid College ID Card.
  • Participants from institutions other than VIT must additionally carry a valid Bonafide Certificate/ OD Letter / Permission Letter issued by their respective institution.
  • Entry into the hackathon will be permitted only after successful verification.

Attendance Requirement

  • All team members are required to be present during registration and verification.
  • Teams are expected to remain available throughout the duration of the hackathon, including mentoring sessions, interim evaluations (if any), and the final project presentation.
  • Every team member is expected to actively contribute to the project. In the event of an emergency or unavoidable absence, the Organizing Committee must be informed immediately.
  • Failure to comply with the attendance requirements may affect the team's eligibility for evaluation or awards, at the discretion of the Organizing Committee.

Participant Requirements

Participants are requested to bring the following:

  • Personal laptop(s)
  • Laptop charger(s)
  • Extension Box(if available)
  • Hardware components and accessories required for their project

The Organizing Committee will provide:

  • Internet connectivity
  • Continuous power supply
  • One extension box per team

Teams requiring additional power outlets are advised to bring their own mini extension boxes.

Meals

Complimentary dinner and breakfast will be provided to all registered participants during the hackathon.

Overnight Policy

  • Participants are free to move within the VIT Chennai campus until 7:30 PM.
  • From 7:30 PM onwards, participants are expected to remain within the designated hackathon venue. Exiting the venue or the campus will not be permitted for security reasons.

Originality & Ethical Conduct

  • Every project submitted must be the original work of the participating team.
  • Previously developed projects, plagiarism, or any form of academic dishonesty will result in immediate disqualification.
  • Participants are expected to uphold the highest standards of professionalism, integrity, and ethical conduct throughout the event.
  • The use of AI-assisted development tools (such as ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot, Gemini, Claude, etc.) is permitted. However, participants must possess a clear understanding of their implementation and should be able to explain any part of their solution during the evaluation.

Code of Conduct

  • Both VIT and Non-VIT participants are required to strictly adhere to the VIT Code of Conduct throughout the event.
  • Any violation of the Code of Conduct or the event guidelines may result in disciplinary action, including immediate disqualification.
  • The detailed VIT Code of Conduct can be accessed here.

Photography & Media Consent

By participating in PARALLAX 2026, participants consent to being photographed, filmed, or recorded during the event. The organizers reserve the right to use such photographs, videos, and recordings for documentation, publicity, promotional activities, and social media, without requiring further consent.

Liability

Participants are solely responsible for the safety and security of their personal belongings, laptops, hardware components, and any other equipment brought to the venue. While reasonable precautions will be taken to ensure a secure environment, the Organizing Committee shall not be held responsible for any loss, theft, or damage to personal property.

Organizer's Decision

The Organizing Committee reserves the right to amend the event schedule, rules, or guidelines whenever deemed necessary in the interest of the event. The decisions of the Organizing Committee and the judging panel shall be final and binding in all matters relating to PARALLAX 2026.

We look forward to your participation and wish you an exciting and rewarding hackathon experience.
Think Beyond. Build Beyond. Welcome to PARALLAX 2026!

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible to participate?

Students from any recognized college or university are eligible to participate.

Is there a registration fee?

Yes. The registration fee is ₹299 per participant.

What is the team size?

Each team must consist of 3 to 5 members.

Can team members be from different colleges, branches, or years?

Yes. Multi-college teams are allowed, and team members can be from different branches and academic years.

What are the hackathon tracks or domains?
  • Aerospace and Space Tech
  • Internet of Things (IoT)
  • Healthcare and Assistive Technologies
  • Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AIML)
  • Communication and Networks
When will the problem statements be released?

Software track problem statements will be released 36 hours before the hackathon, while Hardware track problem statements will be released 108 hours before the hackathon.

What should participants bring to the hackathon?

Participants must bring their laptops, chargers, student ID cards, an extension box, and any additional hardware or components required for their project.

Will food, accommodation, internet facilities, and extension cords be provided?

Yes. Dinner, breakfast, internet facilities, and extension cords will be provided. Bring a mini extension box, as only a limited number of sockets will be provided. Travel and stay facilities (before and after the event) will not be provided for external participants.

How will the projects be evaluated?
  • 15% – Student Review
  • 35% – Faculty Review
  • 50% – Industry Review
What is the prize pool?

Total Prize Pool: ₹60,000+

Will all participants receive certificates?

Yes. All eligible participants will receive an E-Certificate of Participation, while winners will receive additional certificates and prizes.
